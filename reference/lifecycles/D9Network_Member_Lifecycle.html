The mechanics

Three loops keep the cycle turning

A lifecycle is not a funnel with the ends taped together. These are the three feedback paths that decide whether the network compounds or slowly drains.

LOOP ONE

Activation

A member who completes a profile and runs one AI prompt in the first week is a member who renews. Everything in onboarding should point at those two actions and nothing else.

Join → Activate → Engage
LOOP TWO

Referral

Advocates recruit at a fraction of the cost of paid acquisition and bring members who already trust the brand. Each org needs named ambassadors, and their referrals need to be attributable.

Advocate → Discover → Join
LOOP THREE

Recovery

Past-due is not churn yet. A failed card recovered in ten days costs a sequence of three emails; the same member reacquired in six months costs a full acquisition cycle.

Renew → Engage · the dashed line
